## 1단계 — no-text 페이지 생성 (내장 image_gen 도구 사용, 제1원칙)

"bright casual webtoon style, vivid pastel colors (sky blue, cream, coral, mint), soft bold linework, rounded friendly character design, expressive exaggerated faces, flat colors, cheerful bright lighting, clean readable composition"
+ 페이지 지시: single comic page, 2x4 grid (2 columns, 4 rows, equal gutters), reading order left-to-right then next row, absolutely NO text, NO letters, NO speech bubbles anywhere (Korean captions are added later in a separate step).

- P3 (2행 좌, 중간 거리): sealing the dressing room — hands tacking cloth over window edges, two tall oxygen cylinders with rubber breathing tubes on the floor, Mrs. Challenger (tall commanding woman, dark hair with silver streaks in a bun) checking a valve while lean tanned Lord John Roxton (khaki field clothes, small mustache) carries a cylinder

- P8 (4행 우, 여운 마무리): gentle wide morning scene on the village green — people waking one by one, stretching and yawning (a policeman scratching his head, a dog shaking itself); in the near foreground corner Professor Challenger sits calmly in a wicker chair beside the spent oxygen cylinder, eyes closed, small knowing smile
